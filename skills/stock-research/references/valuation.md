# Valuation Standard / 估值标准

## Mandatory elements / 必需元素

A completed research view includes at least one business-appropriate valuation method and one explicit downside case. / 完整研究观点至少包含一种适合业务的估值方法和一个明确的下行情景。

State: / 说明：

- normalized financial base and as-of period; / 标准化财务基础和截至期间；
- key operating and capital assumptions; / 关键经营和资本假设；
- fair-value or scenario range rather than false precision; / 公允价值或情景区间，而非虚假精确值；
- sensitivity drivers and breakpoints; / 敏感性驱动因素和临界点；
- peer and historical context where comparable; / 可比情况下的同业与历史背景；
- why the selected method fits the business. / 所选方法为何适合该业务。

## Method selection / 方法选择

- Multiples: useful for comparable economics and normalized earnings or cash flow. / 倍数法：适用于经济性可比且盈利或现金流已标准化的企业。
- DCF or reverse DCF: useful when long-term cash-flow assumptions can be made explicit. / DCF 或反向 DCF：适用于能够明确长期现金流假设的企业。
- SOTP: useful when segments have materially different economics or peer sets. / SOTP：适用于分部经济性或可比公司显著不同的企业。
- Asset-backed or net-cash-adjusted: useful for financial, holding-company, cyclical, or liquidation-sensitive cases. / 资产价值法或净现金调整法：适用于金融、控股、周期或清算敏感型企业。
- Scenario analysis: required when outcomes are discontinuous or uncertainty is high. / 情景分析：结果非连续或不确定性较高时必须使用。

## Recommendation discipline / 建议纪律

Do not infer an actionable stance from price momentum, technical indicators, social activity, or a screening score alone. / 不得仅凭价格动量、技术指标、社交活跃度或筛选评分推导可行动立场。

Tie the research stance to valuation, evidence quality, downside, trigger, and invalidation. / 将研究立场与估值、证据质量、下行风险、触发条件和失效条件绑定。
