# Research Workflows / 研究工作流

## Single stock / 单一个股

1. Define the question, horizon, and review date. / 定义问题、周期和复盘日期。
2. Build a dated primary-source ledger. / 建立带日期的一手来源账本。
3. Normalize three to five years of financial and operating evidence when available. / 在数据可得时，标准化三至五年的财务和经营证据。
4. Compare reported results, guidance, consensus, and assumptions. / 比较已披露结果、管理层指引、一致预期和分析假设。
5. Value the security with an appropriate method and downside case. / 使用适当方法和下行情景对证券估值。
6. State a falsifiable thesis, direct negative evidence, and invalidation condition. / 说明可证伪论点、直接负面证据和失效条件。
7. Issue a bounded research stance and disclaimer. / 给出有边界的研究立场和免责声明。

## Peer comparison / 同业比较

Use the same period, currency, accounting definitions, and evidence cut-off for every candidate. / 对每个候选使用相同期间、币种、会计定义和证据截止时间。

Compare growth, margins, cash conversion, returns on capital, balance-sheet risk, dilution, valuation, catalysts, and direct negative evidence. / 比较增长、利润率、现金转化、资本回报、资产负债表风险、稀释、估值、催化剂和直接负面证据。

Do not rank companies when material fields are incomparable or missing. / 重要字段不可比或缺失时，不得强行排名。

## Sector or theme basket / 行业或主题篮子

Define inclusion criteria before selecting symbols. / 选择标的前定义纳入标准。

Separate structural drivers from cyclical or narrative momentum, include representative leaders and challengers, and identify shared failure modes. / 区分结构性驱动因素、周期因素和叙事动量，纳入代表性龙头与挑战者，并识别共同失败模式。

End with a research priority list, not a trade list. / 最终输出研究优先级列表，而非交易清单。

## Thesis review / 论点复盘

Start from the prior thesis and ask what changed. / 从既有论点出发，检查发生了什么变化。

Track confirming evidence, disconfirming evidence, catalysts, elapsed time, valuation changes, and the next decisive observation. / 跟踪支持证据、反向证据、催化剂、经过时间、估值变化和下一个决定性观察点。

Retire a thesis when its invalidation condition is met; do not rewrite history to preserve conviction. / 失效条件触发时应终止论点，不得通过改写历史维持信念。
